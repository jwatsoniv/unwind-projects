The Rules — Every Week

  • Warm up first. Never lift or sprint cold. 5 min of light cardio + dynamic moves.
  • Form over weight. If form breaks, the weight is too heavy. Drop it, no ego.
  • Leave 2–3 in the tank. No maxing, no grinding to failure. You're 13 and growing.
  • Eat to grow. You're lean at 106 lb. To add muscle you must eat more — protein every meal (eggs, chicken, milk, peanut butter, Greek yogurt) plus plenty of carbs.
  • Sleep 9 hours. Muscle and speed are built while you sleep. Non-negotiable.
  • Rest days matter. Don't train hard 7 days straight. Recovery is when you get better.
  • Dad spots gym days. Always have a spotter for bench and squat.

The Ravens don't get out-worked. Neither do you. Show up, do the reps, eat, sleep — earn it.
