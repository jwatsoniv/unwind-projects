Good to Know

Winter Rental FAQs

What counts as a monthly rental?

Any stay of 28 nights or longer between November and March qualifies for monthly pricing. Many guests stay two or three months — the longer the stay, the better the rate we can usually offer.

What's included in the monthly rate?

The home is fully furnished with linens, towels, a stocked kitchen, Wi-Fi, and streaming-ready TVs. Utilities are included in most homes for standard usage. We'll spell out exactly what's included before you book.

Do you allow pets?

Many of our homes are pet friendly. Tell us about your pet when you inquire and we'll point you to the right homes.

Are there tax savings on longer stays?

In South Carolina, stays of 90 or more consecutive days are generally exempt from accommodations taxes, which can meaningfully lower the cost of a full-season stay. Ask us for a quote with and without the exemption applied.

Why book direct instead of Airbnb or Vrbo?

Monthly winter rates are only available booking direct — the platforms add service fees and aren't built for long stays. Direct also means you're talking to the local team that actually manages the home.
