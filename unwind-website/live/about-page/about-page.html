Our Story

Built Here. Based Here. Rooted Here.

Unwind was founded in Myrtle Beach with a simple belief: vacation rental ownership should feel rewarding, not overwhelming.

From the beginning, we set out to build a different kind of management company — one that combines personal, hands-on care with the systems and strategy needed to truly maximize a property's performance.

We live where we work. We know our homes. We understand this market at a local level — from seasonality and pricing trends to what guests actually value when choosing where to stay. That insight allows us to operate with intention, not guesswork.

At the same time, we invest heavily in the tools, data, and processes that drive results. Dynamic pricing, listing optimization, and streamlined operations aren't add-ons — they're built into how we manage every property.

Today, we manage a curated portfolio of homes across the Grand Strand. We grow intentionally so we can maintain the level of service, communication, and performance our owners expect.

Because at the end of the day, our goal is simple: to help you get the most out of your home — financially and personally — without the stress of managing it yourself.
